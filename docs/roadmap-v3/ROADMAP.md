## Completion rule

Stage numbering is organisational, not an instruction to serialize unrelated
work. Follow direct child dependencies in the manifest. Epics may depend on
specific boundary children; do not replace those edges with whole-epic gates
that create a cycle during Stage 18's integrated validation.
